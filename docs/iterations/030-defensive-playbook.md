# 030 — Defensive playbook

Gridiron groups defensive calls by coverage family and offers situational shells. Fourth & Long needs a shared catalog for the defensive huddle and sim.

A flat list of named plays would be quick to ship but would make it hard for the huddle to group calls. A full combination generator would offer more combinations than we can balance. Use a curated list with stable IDs and explicit families and shells. A suggested call based on the current game situation will let teams accept a reasonable default with one input.

## Acceptance

- Nine unique calls cover man, zone and blitz in the base, goal-line and prevent shells. The base calls include Cover 1, Cover 2, Cover 3 and a blitz.
- The suggestion selects goal-line defense within five yards, prevent on late downs with at least 15 yards to go and 60 seconds or less, and base coverage otherwise. Goal-line defense takes precedence when both apply.
