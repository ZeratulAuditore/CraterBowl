## Key findings
- **The parity list missed whole systems.** Gridiron shipped them in patches, not in the store blurb: handoffs, punts, FGs, the coin toss, timeouts, the one-minute warning, the competitive ruleset, shield blocking 3.0, play art for every assignment, QB vote, role queue, spectators, chat with a quick-comm wheel, reconnect, the server browser and the 1v1 catching mode.
- **Biggest feel gaps in our code:** the game is pass-only, has no kicking (the kickoff is an automatic reset), the clock stops between every play, players have no moves, and blocking takes no input.
- **The remaster is in open beta with little public detail.** Treat original-game features as the parity reference until the remaster's patch notes appear. The remaster dev says Chaos will be event-only, which is a mild differentiation chance for us.
- **Gridiron's top complaints shape our guardrails:** the game being pulled or paywalled, new players matched against veterans, the Hail Mary meta, toxicity, and long queues.
