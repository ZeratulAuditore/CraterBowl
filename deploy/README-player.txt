CRATER BOWL — arcade online football on the Moon

Run CraterBowl.exe (Windows) or CraterBowl.x86_64 (Linux).
macOS: unzip, right-click Crater Bowl.app -> Open (the build is not notarized yet).

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS
- Host: Play Online -> Host a match. Share the invite code (XXXXX-XXXXX) shown in the lobby.
- Join: Play Online -> paste the code -> Join.
- Hosting opens your router port automatically (UPnP) when your router allows it.
  If friends can't connect, forward UDP port 24565 to your PC, or have someone else host.

CONTROLS (rebind in Settings -> Controls; gamepads work)
WASD move · Shift sprint · Space snap · hold J / left mouse to throw (Ctrl = lob)
E / right mouse tackle · Q play · F route · Tab position · T team · V replay · Esc menu

Your profile, stats and replays live in:
  Windows: %APPDATA%\crater-bowl     Linux: ~/.local/share/crater-bowl
